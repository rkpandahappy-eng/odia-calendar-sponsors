Privacy Policy

Odia Panjika and Calendar 2027 (Android app com.oditechnoapps.odiacalendar)
Effective date: 12 November 2026

Englishଓଡ଼ିଆ

Information you give us

None. The app does not ask for your name, phone number, email address or location, and it needs no account.

Information kept only on your phone

Your language, theme and notification choices, your reminders, and the name you type on greeting pictures are saved only on your phone. We never receive them. They are deleted when you uninstall the app.

Notifications

Notifications for festivals, Ekadashi and your reminders are planned on your phone. Nothing is sent to us to make them work. You can turn them off in the app or in your phone's settings.

Local sponsors

About twice a day the app downloads a list of local business sponsors and their pictures from our page on GitHub Pages (github.io). No personal information is sent. Like any website, GitHub receives the internet (IP) address the request comes from; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. When you tap a sponsor's call, WhatsApp, map or website button, that app or website opens; anything you share there is between you and that business.

Advertisements

The app shows advertisements from Google AdMob. To show ads, limit how often they appear, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, device information, and how ads are viewed or tapped. Google's use of this information is covered by the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's Settings › Google › Ads (or Settings › Privacy › Ads).

Sharing greetings

Greeting pictures are shared only when you choose to share them, through the app you pick (for example WhatsApp).

Selling data

We do not sell personal information, and we do not collect any ourselves.

Children

The app is a general calendar and is not directed at children under 13.

Security

The sponsor list and ads are downloaded over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date, and in the app under More › Privacy Policy.

Contact

For questions about this policy, please write to the developer email address shown on the app's Google Play page.
