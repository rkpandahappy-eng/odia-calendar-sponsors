Sponsor Editor

Add or change the app's local sponsors. Open your sponsors.json, edit, then Download and upload the new file to GitHub. Pictures go in the images folder; type their names here (for example images/shop-poster.jpg). Use a new picture name whenever a picture changes. Animated pictures (.webp or .gif, under 1 MB) work too.

Show sponsors in the app
Off hides every sponsor and your "Advertise here" card on all phones (after you upload the file). Your sponsor list is kept for when you switch it on again.
"Advertise here" card

Shown at the top of the Today page on some days when no sponsor is running, inviting shops to advertise. It only appears once a WhatsApp or phone number is filled in.
